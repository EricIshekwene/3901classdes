How the course works

Most of your grade comes from building web applications in a team. Lectures introduce each technology, individual activities and a quiz check your own understanding, and three exams cover the material.

  • Teams have four or five students. Pick your team by September 2, 2026 in Carmen → People → Project Groups.
  • Projects are submitted as a team in Carmen. Projects 2 through 6 also include a short video and a contribution report.
  • Exams are taken individually. See the dates on the home page.

Grading

The course is worth 100 points, split evenly between coursework and exams.

Coursework

50 points

  • Group work: projects30
  • Individual work: assignments15
  • Attendance5

Exams

50 points

  • Midterm 110
  • Midterm 210
  • Final exam30

To successfully complete the course, students must earn at least 25 out of 50 points in each major assessment component.

Course policies

Late work

Late submissions of group work will be accepted, but with a penalty of 10% off per day late.

Attendance

Students may miss up to three classes and still receive full attendance credit.

Teams

Teams have four or five students. Select your team by September 2, 2026 in Carmen → People → Project Groups.

Exams

You must complete the midterm and final exams yourself, without any external help or communication.

Materials and technology

Textbook

Ruby on Rails Tutorial, 7th Edition by Michael Hartl. Available through the OSU Library’s O’Reilly (Safari) access, as a book and as video.

Software

Setup guides for each tool are on the Resources page.

  • COELINUX: Course Linux environment (via Ivanti VPN and FastX v3)
  • Git: Version control
  • VS Code: Code editor
  • Ruby: Language for scripting and Rails
  • Middleman: Static site generator (Project 4)
  • Rails: Web application framework
  • Postman: Testing web APIs
  • Office 365: Microsoft 365 apps through OSU
